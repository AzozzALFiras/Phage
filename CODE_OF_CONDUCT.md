# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — contributors and maintainers — pledge to make participation in Phage a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We
pledge to act in ways that build an open, welcoming, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address)
  without their explicit permission;
- using this project — or the attack tooling it ships for testing — against
  systems you are not authorized to test, or encouraging others to;
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, commit messages) and when an individual is officially representing
the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported privately to the maintainer at **`azozzalfiras@gmail.com`**. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the
reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
take any action they deem appropriate — from a private warning, to a temporary
ban, to a permanent ban from the community — in response to behaviour they find
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. Maintainers who do not follow
or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may face repercussions as determined
by the project's leadership.
